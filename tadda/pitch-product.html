Why OrderFlow Exists

The Problem We Solve

Custom order businesses — apparel, merchandise, printing — are running on WhatsApp, Excel, and gut feeling.

😓 Without OrderFlow

  • Orders tracked on WhatsApp — things get missed
  • Design files shared over chat — no version control
  • Customer has no visibility — calls admin constantly
  • No payment tracking — chasing advances manually
  • Admin can't tell which vendor has which order
  • Registrations managed on paper or Excel
  • No data — can't tell which customer ordered most

✅ With OrderFlow

  • Every order has a unique ID, status, and timeline
  • Design files stored in cloud — always accessible
  • Customer dashboard — self-service order tracking
  • Razorpay advance payment collected at order
  • Orders assigned to vendors or employees with visibility
  • Brand owner registrations managed with pipeline
  • Full analytics — customers, orders, conversions
Who is this for? Any business that takes custom product orders — apparel printing, merchandise, corporate gifting, packaging, embroidery, promotional products. If you take an order, manage production, and deliver — OrderFlow runs your operations.

Data Privacy & Ownership

Your Data. Your Business. Your Control.

A common and valid concern in B2B SaaS — addressed transparently.

🤔 The Common Concern in B2B SaaS

"The platform provider can see all my orders, customer details, pricing, and business data. What stops them from using it — especially if they're in the same industry?"

This is a legitimate concern. Every serious B2B SaaS buyer should ask this. Here's how OrderFlow answers it.

Option A — Shared Infrastructure
Rule-Based Isolation
  • Data stored in a common Firebase project
  • Firestore security rules prevent platform admin from reading your orders, customers, or registrations
  • Super admin role is restricted to platform settings only — service level, billing, technical config
  • Zero access to /orders, /customers, /registrations enforced at database level
Trust basis: Firestore rules are verifiable — client can review and audit them anytime. Rules are part of the code handover.
Recommended
Option B — Dedicated Project
Your Own Firebase Account
  • Platform deployed into your Google/Firebase account
  • You hold all credentials — API keys, project access, storage
  • We have zero ongoing access unless you explicitly invite us for support
  • Full source code handed over — any developer can maintain it
  • You can revoke our access at any time
Trust basis: We literally cannot access your data — we don't have your account credentials.
✅ What OrderFlow Guarantees — Regardless of Option
🔒 No Data Monetisation
Your business data is never used, analysed, sold, or shared with any third party — including the platform owner.
📦 Full Data Portability
Export all your orders, customers, and registrations as JSON/CSV at any time. You're never locked in.
🔑 Code Ownership
Source code is handed over at the end of Phase 1. You own it. Any developer can read, audit, or modify it.
NDA Available on Request — If required, we are happy to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement before any engagement begins. This is standard practice and we welcome it.
